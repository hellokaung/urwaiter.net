Your Waiter Online Menu App for Koh Tao Restaurants

Your Waiter is a digital menu app for restaurants, bars, and cafes in Koh Tao, Thailand. Guests can open a QR code menu and browse items on their own phone.

What Your Waiter Provides

The app helps food businesses publish a fast, contactless online menu with clear categories, prices, and item photos for better ordering experience.

Built for Local Hospitality

Your Waiter supports multilingual browsing for international travelers and local customers visiting Koh Tao restaurants, beach bars, and coffee shops.
